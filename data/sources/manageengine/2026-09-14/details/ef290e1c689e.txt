ManageEngine Password Manager Pro's MSP Edition allows service providers to centrally manage the privileged passwords of their clients through a fully automated, policy-driven approach. This ensures MSPs are able to offer critical data protection above and beyond their clients’ best practice security guidelines for sensitive information and also helps them demonstrate to their customers that the critical data are being securely handled.
