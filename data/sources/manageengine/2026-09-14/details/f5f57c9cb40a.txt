ManageEngine RecoveryManager Plus is a complete Active Directory and Exchange (on-premises and Exchange Online) backup and restoration solution. Back up all AD objects in your domain and all items in your Exchange environment and restore AD objects and Exchange items to any previous version. Moreover, perform automated incremental backups, compress backups, and define a retention period for your AD and Exchange backups.
