ManageEngine Mobile Device Manager Plus helps enterprises perform mobile device management tasks to configure and secure mobile devices. It simplifies the work of administrators by making them perform mobile device management operations from a central location and is available both on cloud and on-premises.
We have a flexible pricing and it is not necessary to buy only within the prescribed slabs. If the total number of devices in your network do not match with the prescribed slabs, contact sales@manageengine.com to get the pricing for the actual number of devices/technicians that you want to purchase.
