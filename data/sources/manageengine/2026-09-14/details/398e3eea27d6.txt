HelpDesk Onboarding Home » HelpDesk Onboarding
ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus is accepted and appreciated by diversified clients like small, medium enterprise, system integrator etc. and therefore ManageEngine is coming up with various models of Onboarding/Implementing/Migrating (OIM) Service for ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus to suit our diversified clients.

ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus OIM service will help our diversified clients to define process, design workflows, utilize the features effectively, align the processes, workflows with the features and also implement the same for successful Go-Live of our clients.

MangaeEngine ServiceDesk Plus OIM Service will also help our diversified clients to migrate data and configuration from ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus On-Premise to ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus Cloud and also from third party tools.

During the OIM process of ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus, ManageEngine consultants will get involved in various relevant activities with our clients to ensure OIM is smooth and done to the fullest satisfaction of our clients.

Detailed Description Of ManageEngine OIM Activities For ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus.

ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus has four models of OIM
Complete:- ManageEngine will take complete ownership until signing off.
Advanced:- ManageEngine will use the best practice to implement the scope.
Basic:- ManageEngine will implement as defined in the scope.
Migrate:- ManageEngine will migrate data/configuration as defined in scope.
