ManageEngine Malware Protection Plus delivers unified threat detection and remediation across a wide array of malware. Leveraging both traditional antivirus techniques, such as signature-based detection, disinfection, and on-demand scanning, and next-generation methods like exploit detection, DeepAV, machine learning-based behavioral detection, and memory scanning, ensuring enhanced detection capabilities. Furthermore, it offers data recovery and single-click rollback in case of malware infestation.
