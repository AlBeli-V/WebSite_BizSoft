ADManager Plus MSP is a web-based solution for Active Directory, Microsoft 365, Exchange, Google Workspace, and Skype for Business management and reporting needs. It simplifies several routine tasks such as provisioning users, cleaning up dormant accounts, and managing NTFS and share permissions for multiple clients from a single console. ADManager Plus MSP also offers more than 150 prepackaged reports, including reports on inactive or locked-out AD user accounts, Microsoft 365 licenses, and users' last logon times. Build custom workflow structures for each client based on their requirements to handle ticketing and compliance, and automate routine AD tasks such as user provisioning and de-provisioning.
