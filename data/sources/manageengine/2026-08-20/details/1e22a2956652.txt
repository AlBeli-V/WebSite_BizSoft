OpManager Nexus (formerly OpManager Plus) is an IT operations management and observability platform that delivers end-to-end visibility across hybrid infrastructure, network, and applications.

Eg.: 50 pack can be split as per convenience to monitor any combination of Network (Network Devices, Flow Interfaces, Access points, IPs & Switch ports), Server (Servers, Applications and Internet Services) and Storage components - with the total devices under Network, Server and Storage adding up to 50.

OpManager Nexus license options depend on the number of devices to be monitored. The license is inclusive of all the interfaces, nodes or sensors in the device. A device can have any number of interfaces, elements or sensors, unlike our competitors. To know more about how OpManager Nexus is licensed, click here.
