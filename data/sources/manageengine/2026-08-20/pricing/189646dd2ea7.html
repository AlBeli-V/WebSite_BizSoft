What kind of payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept payments via Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. We also accept payments via PayPal (you must have an account with PayPal) and bank transfer for yearly subscriptions. assetexplorer-support@manageengine.com for the on-premises version and assetexplorer-cloud-support@manageengine.com for the cloud version.

Please click here for the complete list of partners.

How does pricing work?

You pay for the number of IT assets you manage.

Who should I contact for sales-related questions?

If you have questions on pricing, please write to sales@manageengine.com.

Do you store customers' credit card information?

We do not store your credit card details. See our privacy policy.

How much does technical support cost?

We do not charge for technical support as it is part of your subscription cost. We offer 24X5 support, except public holidays. You can reach our support through phone and email. Call us toll free at: US : +1 888 720 9500 | Intl: +1 925 924 9500 | Email us at: assetexplorer-support@manageengine.com or assetexplorer-cloud-support@manageengine.com.

Where can I view your terms of service and privacy policy?

You can view the details at terms of service and privacy policy.

Can I purchase more than 10,000 IT assets?

Yes, you can purchase additional IT assets beyond 10,000 if needed. Please contact our team at sales@manageengine.com to purchase additional IT assets.

Is a free trial available for the cloud version of AssetExplorer?

Yes, a 30-day fully functional free trial is available that offers up to 250 IT assets. After the trial expires, your free trial license will be downgraded to the free edition license. If you need more time to evaluate, please contact our team at sales@manageengine.com.
